# Workflow — ANALYZE

## Goal

Turn verified research into a defensible editorial thesis.

Analysis answers: "So what?"

## Inputs

Completed or sufficiently mature research dossier.

## Questions

### Change
- What changed relative to the correct baseline?
- Is the marketed change the technically important change?

### Mechanism
- Why should the change produce the claimed effect?
- Where is the bottleneck before and after?

### Economics
When relevant:
- acquisition cost;
- utilization;
- power;
- memory;
- networking;
- throughput;
- latency;
- cost per workload/output.

### Competition
- Which products are actually comparable?
- Is the comparison current?
- Are ecosystems/software constraints more important than headline silicon?

### Constraints
- What remains the limiting factor?
- What tradeoff was introduced?
- What deployment assumptions are hidden?

### Evidence limits
- What can we not conclude?
- Which claims come only from the vendor?
- Which variables are absent?

## Output

Add a concise `Analysis` section to the research dossier or create `analysis.md` in the story
workspace.

Required:
- one-sentence thesis;
- supporting evidence;
- counterargument or limitation;
- information-gain statement;
- proposed visual/data artifact.

Do not force a provocative thesis if the evidence supports only a descriptive conclusion.
