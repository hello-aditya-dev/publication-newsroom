# Workflow — PUBLISH

## Mandatory gate

This workflow begins only after explicit human approval.

No agent may infer approval from:
- a completed draft;
- a passed fact check;
- urgency;
- a previous approval of a different version.

## Phase 0 publication procedure

1. Confirm the exact approved draft/version.
2. Confirm final headline and dek.
3. Confirm source/fact-check records exist.
4. Prepare publication metadata.
5. Prepare visual/data requirements.
6. If explicitly instructed, transfer only the approved publishable material to the public
   website/CMS workflow.
7. Create `published/YYYY-MM-DD-slug.md` from the publication-record template.
8. Record public URL after deployment.
9. Record meaningful updates/corrections.

## Publication metadata checklist

- slug
- headline
- dek
- author/byline policy
- beat/category
- datePublished
- dateModified
- primary image specification
- alt text
- relevant entities/tags
- canonical URL
- source/citation presentation
- correction/update note when needed

## Distribution preparation

Prepare channel-specific notes only when requested.

Do not spam communities or violate self-promotion rules.

## Post-publication

Measure outcomes when data becomes available.

Do not invent analytics.

Move only durable performance lessons into `memory/LESSONS.md`.
