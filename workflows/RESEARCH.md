# Workflow — RESEARCH

## Gate

Start only for a human-approved story or an explicit human research request.

## Goal

Build an evidence package strong enough that writing becomes synthesis rather than improvisation.

## Procedure

1. Create `research/YYYY-MM-DD-slug/`.
2. Copy/adapt `research/TEMPLATE.md` into `dossier.md`.
3. State the exact research question.
4. Collect primary sources first.
5. Read relevant source pages, docs, papers, filings, releases, and linked technical material.
6. Record source metadata and limitations.
7. Extract confirmed facts separately from interested-party claims.
8. Build numerical/spec tables.
9. Reproduce calculations.
10. Establish the correct comparison baseline.
11. Find contradictions and unknowns.
12. Identify technical mechanism and downstream implications.
13. Identify information-gain opportunities.
14. Decide whether evidence supports the proposed angle.

## Stop conditions

Stop and return to the human editor if:
- central evidence is inaccessible;
- primary evidence contradicts the proposed story;
- the event is materially different from the initial signal;
- no information-gain angle survives research.

## Output

`research/YYYY-MM-DD-slug/dossier.md`

Optional supporting files:
- `sources.md`
- `calculations.md`
- `specs.csv`
- `notes.md`

Do not draft polished article prose during evidence collection unless explicitly requested.
