# Workflow — EDIT

## Goal

Make a verified draft clearer, tighter, more accurate, and more useful without laundering
uncertainty.

## Pass 1 — Structure

- Does the first section contain the real story?
- Does every section advance the argument?
- Can anything be cut?
- Is background placed where needed rather than front-loaded?

## Pass 2 — Evidence

- Does the wording match evidence strength?
- Are vendor claims attributed?
- Are estimates identified?
- Are sources and comparisons current?

## Pass 3 — Technical clarity

- Are terms correct?
- Are units consistent?
- Are comparison baselines valid?
- Are diagrams/tables specified accurately?

## Pass 4 — Style

Remove:
- generic openings;
- clichés;
- press-release adjectives;
- repetitive conclusions;
- excessive bullets/headings;
- empty transition phrases.

## Pass 5 — Headline/dek

Check that neither is stronger than the body.

## Pass 6 — Final gate

Confirm:
- fact-check status PASS;
- unresolved caveats are visible;
- human editor has not yet been bypassed.

## Output

Update the draft and make it **ready for human approval**.

Do not mark it human-approved.
