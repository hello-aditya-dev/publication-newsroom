# Workflow — VERIFY

## Goal

Adversarially validate every consequential claim before final editing.

## Inputs

- research dossier;
- analysis;
- current draft if one exists.

## Procedure

1. Create a fact-check file from `fact-checks/TEMPLATE.md`.
2. Build a claim ledger.
3. Classify fact/vendor claim/estimate/analysis/unknown.
4. Re-open the source for every central claim.
5. Independently recalculate all material math.
6. Check units and denominators.
7. Verify product/model/version/date identity.
8. Audit quotes.
9. Audit benchmark comparability.
10. Search the draft for superlatives and absolute claims.
11. Record conflicts and unsupported claims.
12. Require revision for any central unsupported claim.

## Verification is not copyediting

Do not approve a claim because it "sounds right."

## Gate outcome

- **PASS** — no blocking factual issue.
- **REVISION REQUIRED** — fixable issues remain.
- **BLOCKED** — central thesis cannot currently be substantiated.

A story may proceed to edit only after blocking issues are resolved.
