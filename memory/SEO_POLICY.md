# SEO & Discoverability Policy

## Principle

Search optimization serves readers by making strong journalism discoverable. It must not determine
whether low-value material gets published.

## Search intent

Before targeting a query, ask:
- Does this fit our audience?
- Can we provide a materially better answer?
- Do we have sufficient evidence?
- Can the article remain useful after the initial traffic spike?

## Information gain

A page should add something defensible beyond the existing search results:
- better primary sourcing;
- original comparison;
- calculation;
- changed-spec table;
- benchmark interpretation;
- dataset;
- diagram;
- timeline;
- technical implication;
- reconciliation of conflicting claims.

## Titles

Use accurate, descriptive titles.

Do not manufacture curiosity gaps.

Do not stuff alternate query variants into titles/headings.

## Entities

Use canonical names consistently for:
- companies;
- products;
- models;
- chips;
- standards;
- vulnerabilities;
- technologies.

Entity pages should exist only when they have substantive editorial value.

## Internal linking

Link when it improves understanding:
- background explainer;
- previous generation/product;
- relevant dataset;
- original prior analysis.

Do not create automated internal-link spam.

## Structured data

The public website should eventually support appropriate article metadata such as:
- headline;
- author;
- datePublished;
- dateModified;
- images;
- canonical URL.

Implementation belongs in the website repository, not here.

## News / freshness

For developing stories:
- show original publish time;
- show meaningful update time;
- preserve important correction/update notes.

Do not change dates merely to make old articles appear fresh.

## Scaled content

Do not create large numbers of pages whose only difference is a keyword, product name, location,
or query variation.

Do not produce articles solely because a keyword tool reports volume.

## AI-assisted content

The editorial standard is usefulness, evidence, accuracy, and originality.

Do not attempt to manipulate AI-content detectors or make deceptive authorship claims.

## Discoverability beyond search

Every publishable story should be evaluated for:
- direct/newsletter value;
- Hacker News relevance;
- relevant Reddit/community value;
- X/LinkedIn chart or insight extraction;
- backlink/citation value;
- evergreen search value.

Distribution must respect each community's self-promotion rules.
