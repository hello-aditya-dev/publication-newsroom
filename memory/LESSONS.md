# Reusable Lessons

This is not a diary.

Add a lesson only when human feedback or measured performance establishes a reusable rule that
should change future work.

Format:

## YYYY-MM-DD — Short lesson

**Observed:** What happened.  
**Rule:** What future sessions should do differently.  
**Scope:** Where the rule applies.

---

## Seed editorial rules

### Lead with consequence, not ceremony

**Observed:** Generic announcement openings obscure the technical reason a story matters.  
**Rule:** When evidence supports a clear consequence, lead with it rather than "Company X
announced..." boilerplate.  
**Scope:** Signals, analyses, hardware/model announcements.

### Separate vendor benchmark from independent benchmark

**Observed:** Vendor slides can look like neutral performance facts once paraphrased.  
**Rule:** Preserve who produced a benchmark and its test conditions in both research and prose.  
**Scope:** All benchmark coverage.

### Prefer a killed story to generic filler

**Observed:** A weak story consumes editorial time and dilutes reader trust.  
**Rule:** If research does not uncover information gain, reject or postpone rather than forcing an
article.  
**Scope:** All content.
