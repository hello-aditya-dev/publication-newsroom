# Ranked Story Candidates

## Scoring model

Score each dimension from 0–10.

| Dimension | Weight | Question |
|---|---:|---|
| Technical significance | 20% | Does this materially change a system, capability, constraint, or practice? |
| Reader relevance | 15% | Will our core technical audience care? |
| Information-gain potential | 20% | Can we add something beyond announcement summary? |
| Evidence quality | 15% | Are strong primary sources available? |
| Timeliness | 10% | Is there value in publishing now? |
| Commercial/industry significance | 5% | Does it affect meaningful purchasing/strategy/economics? |
| Search/reference durability | 5% | Can this remain useful or discoverable? |
| Original data/visual potential | 10% | Can we create a table, chart, benchmark, calculation, or dataset? |

**Weighted score:** sum(score × weight).

A high score does not override a sourcing problem.

## Hard fail gates

Reject or hold regardless of numeric score when:
- central claim cannot be verified;
- story is outside scope with no strong justification;
- no meaningful information gain can be found;
- headline would require speculation;
- publishing creates disproportionate harm.
